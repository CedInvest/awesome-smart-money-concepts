# Awesome Smart Money Concepts [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> 🧠 Resources for traders who read price action the institutional way: Order Blocks, Fair Value Gaps, liquidity sweeps, market structure.

Smart Money Concepts grew out of Inner Circle Trader material and the older Wyckoff method. It reads the footprint of large participants through price alone: Order Blocks, Fair Value Gaps, liquidity sweeps, Break of Structure, Change of Character, premium and discount zones.

This list favours what is free and verifiable. Raw data sources and regulators sit next to teaching material, because a trader needs both.

## Market and on-chain data

- [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com) — Prices, market caps and history, with a generous free API.
- [Glassnode](https://glassnode.com) — The on-chain reference: SOPR, MVRV, exchange flows. Several metrics readable without an account.
- [CryptoQuant](https://cryptoquant.com) — Exchange inflows and outflows, miner reserves.
- [DefiLlama](https://defillama.com) — Protocol TVL, no token and no commercial bias.
- [Coinglass](https://www.coinglass.com) — Liquidations, open interest and funding rates. Worth a look before opening any perpetual.
- [Etherscan](https://etherscan.io) — Ethereum explorer: check a contract before you interact with it.
- [mempool.space](https://mempool.space) — Live Bitcoin mempool and fee estimates.

## Regulation and venue selection

Always check which legal entity actually serves you. Several groups route clients to an offshore entity that differs from the licence highlighted in their marketing, which changes both your leverage cap and your recourse.

- [ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu) — European register of MiCA-authorised entities.
- [FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk) — UK register and warning list for cryptoasset firms.
- [Crypto exchange ranking 2026](https://investisseur2-0.com/en/resources/meilleurs-exchanges-crypto-2026.html) — Compared by use case, licences verified one by one.
